Official Announcement

31 July 2026

Aiman Valoryn has decided to withdraw all previously released music from streaming platforms worldwide. Every album and single released to date has been taken down, including:

  • Album: Hell Guard Crazy Bitch — featuring the tracks Hell Guard Crazy Bitch (feat. Young Money $$), I Can't See Bitch (Radio Edit), and Just Little Bitch
  • Album: Like Your Pussy Bitch — featuring the tracks B!TCH, Bad Pussy, and Oh Well
  • Album: Blues
  • Album: Cry
  • Single: Made a Little More Me

This decision was made because the works do not yet meet the standards Aiman Valoryn strives to achieve. Rather than leaving them available in their current form, the choice was made to step back, refine everything thoroughly, and ensure every piece meets the highest possible quality before being shared again. All earnings and royalties accumulated during their availability remain fully secure and unaffected — nothing has been lost.

What Comes Next
While the earlier works are being refined, something new is already on the way. Aiman Valoryn's upcoming album titled Short & Sweet will be released soon, featuring three brand new tracks: Sweet Cloud, Ethereal, and Stillness. This album represents a fresh start — softer, calmer, and crafted with care.

This is not a goodbye. It is a reset. Aiman Valoryn is not stopping music — merely preparing to return with work that is more thoughtful, more refined, and truly ready. Stay tuned.

Pengumuman Rasmi

31 Julai 2026

Aiman Valoryn telah mengambil keputusan untuk menarik balik kesemua karya muzik yang pernah diterbitkan di platform penstriman. Semua album dan lagu ditarik balik, iaitu:

  • Album: Hell Guard Crazy Bitch — lagu Hell Guard Crazy Bitch (feat. Young Money $$), I Can't See Bitch (Radio Edit), dan Just Little Bitch
  • Album: Like Your Pussy Bitch — lagu B!TCH, Bad Pussy, dan Oh Well
  • Album: Blues
  • Album: Cry
  • Single: Made a Little More Me

Keputusan ini dibuat kerana karya-karya tersebut belum mencapai tahap kualiti yang sepatutnya. Daripada membiarkannya begitu sahaja, pilihan dibuat untuk menariknya balik dan menyempurnakan setiap bahagian dengan teliti. Kesemua pendapatan dan royalti yang terkumpul kekal selamat sepenuhnya — tiada apa-apa yang hilang.

Akan Datang
Sementara karya lama disempurnakan, sesuatu yang baharu sedang dalam perjalanan. Album terbaru Aiman Valoryn bertajuk Short & Sweet akan hadir tidak lama lagi, memuatkan tiga lagu baharu: Sweet Cloud, Ethereal, dan Stillness. Album ini adalah permulaan yang segar — lebih lembut, tenang, dan disusun dengan penuh perhatian.

Ini bukan perpisahan, sebaliknya satu permulaan baharu. Aiman Valoryn tidak berhenti mencipta muzik — cuma sedang bersiap untuk kembali dengan karya yang lebih matang dan bermutu tinggi. Sila nantikan.
